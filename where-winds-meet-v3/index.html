Yes. The game is free to play. Optional purchases are cosmetic and are not required to progress.

Can I play with friends on other devices?

Yes. Cross-play lets you team up with friends on PC, console and mobile, and your progress carries over between devices.

Both. Enjoy a full story campaign solo, or explore and fight alongside other players in co-op.
